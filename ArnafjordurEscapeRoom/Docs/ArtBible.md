# Árnafjørður Escape Room — Art Bible (v1)

1. **Silhouette & Materiality:** Grounded Faroese beachside village proportions — weathered driftwood boathouses, dark wet basalt shore stone, mossy turf slopes, and worn concrete quay edges built to read cleanly at phone and desktop aspect ratios.
2. **Atmosphere & Lighting:** Cool North Atlantic coastal sea mist (`#7B8E9C` zenith, `#B4C2C9` horizon) paired with a crisp low-angle sunbreak key (`#FFE6C4`, ~38° elevation) and warm lantern amber (`#E39D54`) so interactive clues stand out immediately against dark slate rock.
3. **Palette Discipline:** Every scene material, ambient gradient, volume tint, and uGUI control pulls from `Arnafjordur.Core.Palette` rather than arbitrary ad-hoc colors.
